Facts

What is real on this site, and what is not

These results are props and are only for beta / demo testing.

  • The score, the "1st in school" rank and the "3rd worldwide" rank are made-up sample values. They do not come from any exam, school or ranking body.
  • The "student library" shown during the search is a list of invented names. No real student database is being searched, and the calculation animation is for show.
  • The discount lines and their rules are examples of how a performance-based fee could work. They have not been awarded by anyone.
  • Aethelgard Academic System is a concept at the idea stage. It is not a registered school or an accredited course.
  • This site does not take payments, and nothing shown here is a bill or a request for money.
  • The standard fee of 160 OMR per month and the adjusted fee of 71 OMR per month are the figures proposed for the concept.
