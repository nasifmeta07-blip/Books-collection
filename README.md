### Notes

- **Source:** The requested site, xwendn.krd, blocks automated access with a Cloudflare bot check. The books were downloaded from the free-books section of
  [qazimuhammad.com/ckb/free-books](https://qazimuhammad.com/ckb/free-books). The physical education book came from
  [martinkurdishorg.wordpress.com](https://martinkurdishorg.wordpress.com/partoken-pdf-pola-12/).
- **Chemistry** is split into two parts because the original 121 MB PDF exceeds GitHub's 100 MB file limit. The pages are unchanged.
- **Physical education** is the official Grade 12 book, but in the Badini (Kurmanji) dialect. A Sorani copy was not found.
- **Art:** No Grade 12 art textbook was found. Ministry announcements mention art education books only for basic grades (7–9).
- **Biology** is a scanned copy (CamScanner) of the student book.
